Oravia

or · AH · vee · ah

ora, to pray  ·  via, the way

Your devotional life, gathered.

God is weaving something beautiful through your life.

A daily place where prayer, Scripture, learning, reflection, and lived experience become meaningful woven threads of how I am trying to live my faith, discern God’s will, and live my purpose.

The vision

The heart of it is one question — how am I becoming the person God is calling me to be? A companion for the whole of your faith journey, drawn from Scripture and the tradition of the Church. It can support discernment, but it never claims to know God’s will for you; you discern the meaning.

More than a prayer app

The things that feed a faith life are scattered — the Rosary you pray, the books you’re reading, the apps you already use, a podcast a friend sent, the people you follow. Oravia gathers them into one place, and links out to how you already pray — like Hallow or Bible in a Year.

  • Prayers
  • Customized devotions
  • Digital & physical resources
  • Voices — people, podcasts, the accounts you follow

Keep your seeking for God.

A private beta — still taking shape. No account yet: everything you add stays on your own device, in this browser. It doesn’t sync across devices, and nothing is sent to a server.
