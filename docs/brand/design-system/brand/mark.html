Oravia · Design System

The mark

One gold ring doing three jobs: the O of Oravia, a compass bezel, and — because the vertical axis runs long and the crossbar sits above centre — a cross. The compass is what you see. The cross is what you notice a moment later.

Geometry

Canvas
100 × 100, square
So it drops into favicons, app icons and avatars without letterboxing.
Ring
cx 50 · cy 50 · r 33 · stroke 6
The O, and the compass bezel.
Vertical
N 20 → S 80 (60 units)
The long axis. This is what makes the cross.
Horizontal
W 29 → E 71 (42 units)
Deliberately 70% of the vertical — never equalise them.
Crossbar
y 42, above centre
37% down the vertical. Centre it and the cross turns into a crosshair.
Shafts
vertical ±4.2 · horizontal ±3.6
The slight difference keeps the arms from reading as a plus sign.

Two cuts

Below 20px the regular cut dissolves — the ring goes hairline and the arms vanish. Swap to the small cut, which carries a heavier ring and shorter arms.

Rules

DoGive it clear space of at least a quarter of its diameter on every side.
DoSwitch to the small cut below 20px — heavier ring, shorter arms.
DoUse one colour. The mark is monoline by design.
Don'tEqualise the arms, or drop the crossbar to centre — that is a crosshair, not a cross.
Don'tFill the ring, add a second ring, or set the mark on a busy photo.
Don'tStretch it. The canvas is square and the proportions are load-bearing.
