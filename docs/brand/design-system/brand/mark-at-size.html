What breaks, and where

The stem
Runs to y=122 in a 122-tall viewBox — it touches the exact bottom edge. In any square icon tile it either clips or collides with the mask. Needs padding built into the artwork.
The centre dot
r=3 on a 100-wide canvas is 3% of the width. Below roughly 32px it stops resolving as a dot and turns into a smudge, then disappears.
Fill vs stroke
The flame is filled solid; the circle is a 2.4 stroke. They scale at different optical weights, so the flame goes heavy and the ring goes thin as size drops — the mark loses its balance rather than just getting smaller.
The opening
The gap at the top of the O is ~20 units wide. At large sizes it reads as deliberate — a journey still unfolding. At 24px and below it reads as a printing error.
Aspect ratio
122 tall on a 100 canvas. Everything square — favicons, app icons, avatars — will letterbox it, shrinking the mark further inside an already-tight tile.

None of this makes the mark wrong — it is genuinely lovely at card and header scale, which is where you have used it. It means the app icon likely wants a separate, simplified cut of the same idea rather than the same file scaled down: heavier ring, no centre dot, stem shortened or dropped, square canvas with real padding.
