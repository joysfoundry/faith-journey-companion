The mark as the O

The mark was drawn as the O of Oravia from the start. This sets it back into the word it came from. Every specimen below is generated from the same geometry as public/oravia-mark.svg, and the ring is fitted to the measured cap height of Cormorant Garamond at that size — not to an em ratio, which is how a lockup ends up three percent off and subtly wrong.
