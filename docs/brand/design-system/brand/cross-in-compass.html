One thing to watch

A cross centred in a circle is also the Celtic cross, and at small sizes with equal arms it can read as a crosshair or a target. Both are avoided the same way: keep the horizontal clearly shorter than the vertical, and keep the crossbar above centre. 5a and 5b both do this; 5 as originally drawn does not, which is why no cross reads in it.

If you want a recommendation: 5a. It is the version where someone sees a compass, and a moment later sees the cross — better than being handed the cross immediately. It also holds at 16px, which 5c and 5d do not.
