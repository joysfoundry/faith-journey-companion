Compass × flame

Four ways to fuse the two marks you already own. Each is shown large down to 16px, then on a navy tile at app-icon, home-screen and favicon size — because the size test is what kills most logo ideas, and it should kill them early.

The compass keeps its silhouette; its north point becomes the flame. You still read a compass instantly, and the thing you steer by is prayer.

The honest fifth option

Don’t fuse them. Let the compass be the app icon — it is square, solid, and survives 16px, which is exactly what an icon must do — and let the flame be the wordmark lockup, where it sits above “Oravia” on cards, the flyer and the app header. Two marks in one system is normal; a monogram and a full logo do different jobs. The risk of fusing is ending up with something that is neither a good compass nor a good flame.

These are quick sketches, not finished artwork — the curves would need real drawing time once you pick a direction. Judge the idea and the small sizes, not the polish.
