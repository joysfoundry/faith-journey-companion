The gold O

One ring that has to do two jobs: read as a compass bezel, and read as the O of Oravia. Six ways to mark it. Each is shown large down to 16px, on navy at app-icon and favicon size, and in the wordmark lockup — the 16px column is the one that decides.

If you want a recommendation: 1 for the app icon and favicon, 3 for the wordmark lockup. The ticked ring survives anything and keeps the O unmistakable; the flame version carries the meaning you have already built, and it only ever has to work large. That is one mark with two cuts, not two logos.

These are geometric sketches, not final artwork — stroke weights and the flame curve want real drawing time once you choose. Judge the idea and the small sizes.
