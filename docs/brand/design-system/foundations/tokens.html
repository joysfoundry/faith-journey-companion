Inherited sets

The --sidebar-* block mirrors these one-for-one and needs no independent decisions — sidebar is parchment, sidebar-primary is primary. The five --chart-* slots stay a separate categorical ramp; they are not brand colors and should not be retuned to match.

The one collision to watch

Burgundy and destructive are both dark reds. They stay legible only if burgundy is never a filled button: it appears as small caps, a rule, or a season dot. A filled red rectangle means one thing in this app — something is about to be deleted.
