Oravia · Design System

The published palette

What is live in the beta right now — the cool blue-grey palette, with its blues and golds tuned to match the mark. Surfaces are deliberately untouched: this is not the warm Illuminated palette, which stays staged in tokens.css until you choose to ship it.

Why tune at all

The mark is built on Marian blue at hue 264 and antique gold. The live palette sat at hue 256–258 with a brighter, yellower gold — close enough to look like a mistake rather than a choice. Sixteen tokens moved — the blues, the golds, and the chart and sidebar slots that mirror them. Nothing else did.
