Choose A if

You want the app to stay quiet and let prayer text be the loudest thing on screen. Gold stays rare, so it still means something when it shows up on “Now.” The cost: someone opening the app cold may not register a Marian palette at all — it reads as a tasteful, fairly generic app, and the gold work is nearly invisible.

Choose B if

You want it to look unmistakably devotional at a glance — closer to an illuminated manuscript, and much closer to your printed flyer and About card. The cost: gold on every section header adds up fast on a long screen, and once gold is everywhere the gold “Now” pill stops standing out.

If you want a recommendation: start at A and move one element at a time toward B. The single highest-value borrow from B is the gold top rule on the Word card — it marks the day’s Scripture as the thing that matters without touching any other surface. The one to avoid longest is the gold section-header band, which is what makes B feel heavy.
